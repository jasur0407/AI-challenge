## AI Challenge 2026 task completions

### Qualification Stage tasks

#### Apartment price prediction

Predicting the prices of apartment based on their - area, floor, distance to center properties. During the process GradientBoostingRegressor, RandomForestRegressor, XGBoostRegressor, CatBoostRegressor models were tested. GradientBoostingRegressor demonstrated the least MAE (Mean Absolute Error) of 780, while other models showed performed worse. In the official AI Challenge page the model scored 0.48 compared to the highest score of approximately 0.555.
